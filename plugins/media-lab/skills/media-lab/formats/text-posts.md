# Text posts: LinkedIn, X, threads

## LinkedIn post
```
Line 1-2: concrete result or moment (a number, a name, a thing that happened)
(blank line)
3-6 short paragraphs, 1-2 lines each: what happened, what was hard, what changed
One specific takeaway, said once
A real question, or nothing
```
- Under ~1,300 characters usually; shorter is fine.
- Native media (image, short video, PDF document) beats a link. Link in the first comment.
- Tag only people who were actually involved. 0-3 hashtags.
- Reply to early comments; the thread is part of the post.

## X / thread
- Tweet 1 stands alone and carries the whole promise.
- One point per tweet; numbers like 1/ are optional.
- Visual every 2-3 tweets if you have them.
- Last tweet: the summary or the ask; link in a reply.

## Launch post (product, project, event)
1. What it is in one plain sentence.
2. Who it's for and the one thing it does better.
3. Proof: demo clip, screenshot, number.
4. How to try it (link in comment on LinkedIn).
5. Thanks to real people involved, by name, only with their OK.
