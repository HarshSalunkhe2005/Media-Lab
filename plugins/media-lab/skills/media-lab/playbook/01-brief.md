# 1. Brief

No asset gets made before this exists. Five minutes here saves the whole edit.

```
Piece:        <working title>
Platform:     <Shorts | Reels | TikTok | IG carousel | LinkedIn | X | YouTube long | event story>
Audience:     who exactly, and what they already know
One idea:     the single thing they should remember (one sentence, no "and")
Why now:      trend, event, launch, question people keep asking
Goal metric:  views from non-followers | saves | sends | comments | clicks | signups
Voice:        Harsh personal | brand/team account (name it) | faceless channel persona
Proof:        what we can show on screen (footage, numbers, demo, screenshots) and the sources for any facts
Constraints:  length, deadline, assets we have, things we must not say
Done means:   e.g. "60 s vertical MP4 + cover + caption, reviewed by Harsh"
```

Checks before moving on:
- If the one idea needs "and", it is two pieces.
- If there is nothing to show (no proof), the piece will be talk over stock. Find proof or change the idea.
- The goal metric picks the format: reach from strangers favours Shorts/Reels; saves favour carousels and lists;
  comments favour opinions and questions; clicks favour LinkedIn documents and threads with the link in a reply.
