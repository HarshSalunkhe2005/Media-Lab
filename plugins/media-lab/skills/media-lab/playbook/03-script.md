# 3. Script

Write for the ear and the thumb: short sentences, one beat per line, a visual for every line.

## Short vertical (under 60 s)
- Line 1 is the hook: under 12 words, lands in under 2 seconds, opens a gap. No intro, no greeting, start mid-action.
- 95 to 135 spoken words for ~45 to 55 s at a brisk TTS pace; 70 to 90 words for ~30 s.
- Change something on screen every 2 to 4 seconds (cut, zoom, new caption block, overlay).
- Payoff in the last beat. End on a line that loops into the start or invites a comment.
- Facts only from cited sources; opinions are framed as the take.
