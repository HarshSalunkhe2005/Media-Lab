# Critique rubric

Score honestly, 0 to 10, and list concrete fixes for anything under 7. Pretend you are a tired viewer with the thumb
already moving.

| Axis | 10 looks like | Fails when |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | I need the next 3 seconds | greeting, logo, context before the point |
| Clarity | one idea, said plainly | two ideas, jargon, vague nouns |
| Retention | something changes or escalates every few seconds | a static stretch over 5 s, a list with no countdown |
| Payoff | the promise is kept, ideally with a twist | answer withheld, or obvious |
| Proof | claims shown or cited | "studies show", unsourced numbers |
| Originality | our angle, our footage or our words | rehash of a viral video, borrowed script |
| Voice | sounds like the named person or account | AI tells (see `voice.md`) |
| Packaging fit | piece delivers exactly what title/cover promise | bait and switch |

Verdict:
- **ship**: Hook >= 7, Retention >= 7, Originality >= 6, Proof >= 6, no policy or accuracy risk
- **kill**: unsafe, defamatory, cannot be made original, or no proof exists
- **revise**: everything else (max two rounds)
