# Taste: reads as AI vs reads as human

Read this before any carousel, quote or poetry post, thumbnail, poster or cover. Structure alone isn't enough:
a correct layout with generated glows still looks machine-made, and people scroll past it.

## Do / don't

| Reads as AI (don't) | Reads as human (do) |
|---|---|
| Procedural gradients, glows, bokeh blobs, fake moons and stars | A real photo or a real texture (paper, film grain, fabric, a wall) from `recipes/stock-photos.md` |
| Gold italic serif on a black or navy gradient | Off-white or ink-dark text in one plain typeface, colour taken from the photo |
| Two or three typefaces, drop shadows, outer glow, ornaments, dividers, flourishes | One typeface, two sizes at most, no effects. Use a shadow only if the text is unreadable without it. |
| Text dead-centre on every slide | Text sits in the photo's negative space (sky, wall, table, water) and moves with it |
| Perfectly centred, symmetrical subject | Real framing: the subject off-centre, a crop that cuts something, a slight tilt left as shot |
| Title Case Headlines Everywhere | Sentence case, like someone typed it. Poetry keeps its own line breaks. |
| Every slide uses the same template | A deliberate system with variation: full-bleed photo, then text on paper, then photo again |
| Over-smooth, saturated, HDR-looking images | Natural colour, some grain, a shadow that is actually dark |
| Emoji bullets, sparkle icons, "✨" accents | No decoration. If something needs emphasis, use the words. |
| Filler lines that rhyme by force ("journey", "soul", "embrace") | Specific concrete nouns: the bus stop, the chai cup, 6:40 PM |

## Quote and poetry posts
- One poem or quote per post, or one stanza per slide. Credit the author by name if it isn't Harsh's.
- Line length: break where a person would pause, not where the box ends.
- Match the photo to the feeling, not to the literal word (a poem about waiting gets an empty platform, not a clock).

## Thumbnails and covers
- A real face, object or moment, photographed. Generated "epic" scenes read as fake at thumbnail size.
- Short words in a bold, plain sans serif. Never neon outlines or chrome text.

## AI-look smell test (run before showing anything; all six must pass)
1. **Source**: is the background a real photo or texture, not a generated gradient or blob? If generated, would a person believe it was shot?
2. **Restraint**: one typeface, no glows, shadows, ornaments or sparkles (or a stated reason for each)?
3. **Placement**: does the text sit in the image's negative space, avoiding faces and the subject?
4. **Cliché**: none of these: gold on black, purple-blue gradients, lens-flare moons, centred italic serif quote boxes?
5. **Voice**: do the words read like a person (sentence case, concrete nouns, no filler rhyme)? Run `humanize` on captions.
6. **Variation**: across the set, at least two layout variants, and nothing that looks like one template stamped five times?

If any point fails, fix it before showing Harsh. Say which point you fixed.
