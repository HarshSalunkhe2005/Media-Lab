# Voice

**For anything posted under Harsh's name, run the global `humanize` skill on the final copy.** That skill is the source of
truth for his voice and the AI-tell blacklist; this file only adds platform specifics.

Platform notes:
- **Spoken scripts**: contractions, short sentences, words people actually say. Read it out loud once; cut anything you stumble on.
- **Short-video captions/descriptions**: first line repeats the promise in different words; 1 to 3 hashtags max, specific ones.
- **LinkedIn**: first line is a concrete result or a specific moment, not a lesson. Short lines, white space, no emoji bullets, no
  "Here's what I learned:" scaffolding. Link goes in the first comment.
- **X/threads**: tweet 1 must stand alone; each tweet one point; last tweet sums up or asks.
- **Team/brand accounts** (e.g. a college league page): follow that account's existing tone and templates; no personal "I".

Never: fake urgency, invented stats, engagement bait ("comment YES"), fake testimonials, impersonating a real person or brand.
