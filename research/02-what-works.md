# What makes short-form content work: evidence, with honest confidence levels

Almost everything published on this is from tool vendors and creator blogs, not controlled studies, and the numbers
disagree. So each claim below carries a level:

- **strong**: platform's own statement or consistent across many independent sources
- **medium**: repeated by several sources, plausible, not verified
- **weak**: single vendor, unsourced number. Use as a hypothesis to test on your own analytics, never as a fact.

Your own channel analytics beat every number here.

## Short vertical video (Shorts, Reels, TikTok)

| Claim | Level | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| The opening 1 to 3 seconds decide most swipes. Exact thresholds (70%, 80%) vary by source. | strong (direction), weak (numbers) | [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/youtube-shorts-length), [gofaceless](https://www.gofaceless.ai/en/blog/high-retention-youtube-shorts-attributes), [eliro](https://eliro.pro/blog/short-form-video-hooks-script-guide-2026) |
| Hooks that hold: a stated outcome, visible motion, an unfinished number, a contrarian fact. Hooks that lose: logo intros, greetings, "in this video". | medium | [shortsfast](https://shortsfast.com/blog/faceless-shorts-hooks-retention-2026/), [ugccopilot](https://ugccopilot.ai/hooks/youtube-shorts/) |
| On-screen text that promises a payoff, plus a small zoom, lifted 3-second hold from 54% to 71% in one A/B test. | weak (single case) | [eliro](https://eliro.pro/blog/short-form-video-hooks-script-guide-2026) |
| Many top Shorts sit around 25 to 35 seconds; in a 30 s Short each second is about 3% of watch time. | weak | [Toptal](https://www.toptal.com/creator/post/youtube-shorts-length) |
| Reading the retention curve: an early drop is a hook problem, a late drop is an ending problem. | medium (useful diagnostic) | [shortgenius](https://shortgenius.com/blog/youtube-shorts-best-practices) |
| Burned-in captions raise watch time (one vendor says 18%). Most viewers watch muted at least sometimes. | medium (direction), weak (number) | [chatcut](https://chatcut.io/blog/how-to-make-youtube-shorts) |
| A loop ending (last line flows into the first) increases rewatches. | medium | [saturaai](https://saturaai.com/blog/how-to-go-viral-on-youtube-shorts) |

## Long-form lessons that transfer

- The leaked MrBeast production handbook (2024, authenticity confirmed by two former producers, not by the company)
  treats the first minute as the most important because that is where most viewers leave, and treats every second
  as something that has to hold attention. [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2024/09/17/mrbeast-internal-production-guide-leaked-key-points/),
  [Passionfruit](https://passionfru.it/mrbeast-leaked-document-81151/). Level: medium. The same coverage describes a punishing work culture; we take the
  retention thinking only.

## Rules Media Lab adopts from this

1. Hook in the first 2 seconds, spoken and on screen.
2. Captions on everything vertical.
3. One idea per Short, per slide, per post.
4. Package (title, thumbnail or cover, first line) before production, not after.
5. Measure: read the retention curve and change one thing per upload.
