# Inspiration: archetypes and teardown method

We study **techniques**, never copy a creator's script, footage, thumbnails or catchphrases. Teardowns describe what a
format does, in our own words. Demo content in this repo uses fictional names only.

## Archetypes worth studying (search these to find live examples)

| Archetype | Typical examples to look up | Technique to take |
|---|---|---|
| Spectacle long-form | MrBeast | Escalating stakes every 30 to 60 s; the promise in the title is shown in the first seconds. |
| Productivity / explainer YouTube | Ali Abdaal, Thomas Frank | Clear chapter structure, B-roll on every claim, packaging decided before filming. |
| Packaging strategist | Paddy Galloway (consultant breakdowns) | Title and thumbnail as one idea; the thumbnail shows what the title does not say. |
| Science short-form | Kurzgesagt-style motion, Veritasium Shorts | One surprising fact, visual proof, loop back to the question. |
| Tech / dev short-form | Fireship ("in 100 seconds") | Dense, fast narration over code and diagrams; jokes as pacing breaks. |
| Faceless story Shorts | Reddit-story and history-fact channels | Hook as an unresolved situation; captions carry the whole video. |
| LinkedIn builders | Founder and engineer accounts posting build logs | A concrete number or result in line 1; short lines; one lesson. |
| Carousel educators | Design and finance educators on Instagram | Slide 1 is a promise, slide 2 is a second hook, last slide asks for a save or send. |

## Teardown template (use for any video or post)

```
Piece:         <link>            Format: <Short | Reel | carousel | post | long-form>
Hook (0-2 s):  what is said / shown / written
Promise:       what the viewer expects to get
Structure:     beats with timestamps or slide numbers
Retention:     the devices used (open loop, list countdown, visual change every N s, captions style)
Payoff:        where and how the promise is kept
Ending:        loop / CTA / question
Packaging:     title, thumbnail or cover, first line
Steal-able technique (in our words):
Do not copy:   script lines, footage, art, names
```

Save finished teardowns in `research/teardowns/` (one file each) and link the technique into `hooks/` or `formats/`.
